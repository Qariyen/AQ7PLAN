AQ7 ZOR: put it on your iPhone home screen

An iPhone only installs a real full-screen app from a website address (https).
This folder is that website. It needs a free home once. It takes about 3 minutes.

1. On a computer, go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and make a free account.
2. Unzip this file and drag the whole folder onto the page.
3. You get an address like something.netlify.app.
4. On your iPhone, open that address in Safari.
5. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
6. Open AQ7 from your home screen once while online. After that it works offline.

Your progress stays on your phone. Use Codex (the star, top right) and Copy backup
before you change phone.
